01 / Business overview

Regular visits.
Simple ways to join.

Passes and memberships are the main income. Drinks, useful merchandise and small events can add to the experience and support the business.

Who it is for

  • A /
    People making time for themselves

    Local residents and working adults who want a regular pause in their week.

  • B /
    People who enjoy staying active

    Runners, gym members and recreational sports groups looking for a shared wellness space.

  • C /
    Friends and small communities

    People who want a calm place to spend time together.

How the studio earns

  1. Studio passes + membershipsSingle visits and repeat-visit passes for sauna and cold-pool access.
  2. Juice + smoothiesA short menu of fresh drinks. Start with a few well-made options and clear ingredients.
  3. Small events + group passesHosted wellness sessions, club gatherings and private groups during quieter periods.
  4. Useful studio merchandiseTowels, bottles and simple clothing. Start with preorders to keep stock costs low.

03 / Improvements

Make the routine
worth returning to.

Put the member experience first. Test a simple version, listen to feedback and improve it before adding more.

01 / First visit

Help people feel welcome.

Give a short introduction to the facilities, clear signs and an easy way to ask for help. Keep the experience relaxed, with no pressure to try everything.

02 / Atmosphere

Test the full experience.

Invite a small trial group to comment on lighting, music, scent, privacy and comfort. Offer quiet and fragrance-free periods before setting the final schedule.

03 / Space

Protect a place to rest.

Make room for dry seating, drinking water and an uncluttered route through the studio. Check that seating fits the next measured layout.

04 / Routine

Make coming back easy.

Show busy periods, pass validity and visit history clearly in the app. Try regular morning and evening community sessions, with optional reminders.

05 / Refreshments

Keep the menu small.

Start with four to six drinks, clear ingredients and consistent preparation. Test a pass-and-drink option and measure waste before expanding.

06 / Progress

Measure what brings people back.

Track repeat visits, member feedback, busy times, drink sales and cash left each month. Use confirmed sales and a small pilot before making bigger commitments.
